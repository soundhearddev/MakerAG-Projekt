Model No. : SE-NS-108

 
  Desk Top 8 Ports 10/100 Fast Ethernet Switch  
     
   
   
   
 

Features :


SE-NS-108 unmanaged DeskTop switch, with (8) 10/100BASE-TX ports, is one of the most popular switches in our product line. It is known for its exceptional performance and quality. The SE-NS-108 is compact in size, so that it can be placed anywhere on a desktop.Its power connector and ports are conveniently located in the rear to avoid messy cables on the desk.

  • Fully compliant with IEEE802.3 (10BASE-T), IEEE802.3u (100BASE-TX)

  • Straight and cross cables auto detect, Uplink port ready for any of the ports

  • Broadcast storm protection supported, Dynamic buffer Assign and network load balance, Avoids package loss

  • 8 10/100Mbps fast Ethernet ports

  • External power adaptor, low consumption

  • Full and half duplex modes supported

  • 1K MAC address

  • MAC address auto learning and refreshing

  • 2Mb fast packet buffer

  • Mini Plastic Skin, Desktop Mount
